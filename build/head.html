How to play

Light up every receptor 🎯 with exactly its color. Pick a piece below, tap a square to place it (the picker deselects after each placement). Tap a placed piece to remove it. Light recomputes instantly.

Controls

  • ✥ Drag a placed emitter, mirror, splitter, or combiner to rotate it to any angle.
  • 🔍 Pinch or mouse wheel to zoom.
  • 🔄 Drag the background (off the board) to orbit around the board.
  • 🎨 Emitters and Receptors live in drawers — tap the group to expand it upward, then tap a color to pick it.

Pieces

  • 🔆🔴🟢🔵 Emitters — fire white or colored light along their aim.
  • 🪞 Mirror — reflects light across its line, at any angle.
  • 🔺 Splitter prism (drag to aim) — fans light around its aim: red −60°, yellow −30°, green straight, cyan +30°, blue +60°. White paints the whole fan. It undoes the combiner: yellow splits back into red+green, orange into red+yellow, and so on. A lone red, green, cyan, or blue band is duplicated into a pair at ±30°.
  • 🔻 Combiner prism — beams arriving off its arrow merge into one mixed-color beam exiting along the arrow. Beams near the arrow pass straight through.
  • Receptors — the goal. Needs exactly its color.
  • 🧱 Wall — blocks light. Emitters are opaque too.

Colors

There are exactly 10 colors: five spectral bands (red · yellow · green · cyan · blue), the four in-betweens (orange · lime · teal · azure), and white. The combiner blends whatever arrives into the color at the middle of its spectrum: red + green = yellow, red + yellow = orange, yellow + green = lime, and so on. A mix that doesn't land on a real color (like red + blue) fizzles into nothing — white comes only from red + green + blue or from all five bands at once. The splitter reverses it: orange splits back into red and yellow, yellow back into red and green.

Board as JSON

Only fixed 📌 pieces are exported unless you tick the box — handy for saving work-in-progress while designing. Non-fixed pieces are marked "fixed": false so they come back unlocked. Save the JSON under build/levels/, then set id, name, hint, inv.
